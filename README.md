# 🎨 Carry — Personal Website

Welcome to my corner of the web! I am a multidisciplinary researcher exploring **camera imaging, color science, and image signal processing**. My path began in HVAC engineering and building energy systems, then led me to computational color constancy, cross-camera color consistency, tone mapping, and RAW/DNG processing.

🔗 **Visit the website:** [hurricane-k.github.io](https://hurricane-k.github.io/)

## ✨ What you'll find

- **About** — my research journey from HVAC to camera imaging.
- **Work & Education** — the places and people behind that journey.
- **Selected Publications** — research in color imaging and building systems, with a button to explore the full list.
- **GitHub Projects** — open code and paper reproductions in hyperspectral imaging, color constancy, and camera color correction.
- **News & Awards** — recent milestones and recognition.

The design keeps things clean and readable, with subtle rainbow accents inspired by color science. It adapts to both desktop and mobile screens. 🌈
